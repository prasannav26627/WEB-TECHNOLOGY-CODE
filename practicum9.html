Self-Service Knowledge Base

Article #1042: Secure VPN Connection

Employees working remotely must connect to the corporate VPN and complete MFA.

Step 1: Open the VPN application.
Step 2: Enter your corporate username.
Step 3: Complete Multi-Factor Authentication.
Step 4: Confirm that your VPN connection is active.

Article #1043: Browser Cache Troubleshooting

Clear browser cookies and cache when websites display outdated information.

Step 1: Open browser settings.
Step 2: Select Privacy and Security.
Step 3: Choose Clear Browsing Data.
Step 4: Reload the website.
