    IT Help Center

    Search the troubleshooting guides or pick a category. Open any guide to see the steps.

    VPN keeps disconnecting Network 5 min

    The VPN drops every few minutes or asks you to sign in again and again.

    • Check that your home internet is stable by opening a website without the VPN.
    • Close the VPN app completely and open it again.
    • Choose a different VPN gateway from the server list.
    • Update the VPN app to the latest version.
    • If it still drops, raise a ticket with the time of the last disconnect.
    Still not fixed? Raise a ticket
    Wi-Fi is connected but there is no internet Network 4 min

    The Wi-Fi icon shows connected, but websites and email will not load.

    • Turn Wi-Fi off and on again on your device.
    • Forget the office network and join it again with your credentials.
    • Restart your laptop to refresh the network settings.
    • Try another device on the same network to see if others are affected.
    Still not fixed? Raise a ticket
    Laptop will not turn on Hardware 6 min

    Nothing happens when you press the power button.

    • Plug in the original charger and check that its light is on.
    • Hold the power button for 15 seconds, then release it.
    • Remove all USB devices and docking stations.
    • Press the power button again and wait one minute.
    • If there is still no sign of life, request a replacement through a P2 ticket.
    Still not fixed? Raise a ticket
    Screen is flickering or blank Hardware 5 min

    The display flashes, shows lines or stays black while the computer is running.

    • Check that the monitor cable is firmly connected at both ends.
    • Try a different cable or port (HDMI or DisplayPort).
    • Lower the refresh rate in the display settings.
    • Connect the monitor to another computer to find out which device is faulty.
    Still not fixed? Raise a ticket
    Printer is not printing Hardware 5 min

    Documents are sent to the printer but nothing comes out.

    • Check the printer display for paper, toner or jam messages.
    • Open the print queue and cancel any stuck jobs.
    • Make sure the correct printer is selected as the default.
    • Restart the printer and print a test page.
    Still not fixed? Raise a ticket
    Application is frozen or not responding Software 3 min

    A program stops reacting to clicks or closes by itself.

    • Wait one minute in case the app is busy saving.
    • Open Task Manager and end the frozen application.
    • Restart the application and reopen your file.
    • Restart the computer if it happens again.
    Still not fixed? Raise a ticket
    Website shows outdated information Software 3 min

    A web page still shows old data after it has been updated.

    • Press Ctrl + F5 to reload the page without the cache.
    • Open browser settings and choose Privacy and Security.
    • Select Clear Browsing Data and tick cookies and cached files.
    • Reload the website.
    Still not fixed? Raise a ticket
    Reset a forgotten password Access 4 min

    You cannot sign in because you forgot or lost your password.

    • Open the company sign-in page and choose Forgot password.
    • Enter your work email address.
    • Approve the request with your registered phone or authenticator app.
    • Create a new password with at least 12 characters.
    • If your account is locked, raise a P3 ticket for a manual unlock.
    Still not fixed? Raise a ticket
    Set up Multi-Factor Authentication (MFA) Access 6 min

    MFA adds a second check when you sign in, so a stolen password alone is not enough.

    • Install the company authenticator app on your phone.
    • Sign in to the security page and choose Add sign-in method.
    • Scan the QR code with the authenticator app.
    • Enter the six-digit code to confirm the setup.
    • Save your backup codes in a safe place.
    Still not fixed? Raise a ticket
    I received a suspicious email Security 2 min

    An email asks for your password, money or urges you to click a link quickly.

    • Do not click any link or open any attachment.
    • Use the Report Phishing button in your email app.
    • Delete the message from your inbox and trash.
    • If you already clicked or entered a password, change it at once and raise a P1 ticket.
